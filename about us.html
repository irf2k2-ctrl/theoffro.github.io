About OffrO

Developed and operated by MIB Tech Solutions

OffrO is a local discovery platform developed and operated by MIB Tech Solutions. Our mission is to connect customers with trusted local businesses while helping merchants grow through digital visibility, promotions, rewards, and local marketing.

Our Vision

To make discovering nearby businesses, products, services, and exclusive offers simple, rewarding, and accessible for everyone.

What OffrO Offers

  • Discover nearby stores and local businesses
  • Browse products from trusted merchants
  • Find exclusive offers and promotions
  • Earn rewards through QR code scanning
  • Save your favourite stores
  • Explore businesses across multiple cities

For Customers

OffrO helps customers discover nearby restaurants, cafés, retail stores, pharmacies, salons, supermarkets, and many other local businesses. Users can explore products, compare offers, collect rewards, and support local merchants.

For Merchants

OffrO provides merchants with tools to manage stores, publish products, create offers, display promotional banners, increase visibility, and attract more nearby customers.

Our Mission

Our goal is to strengthen local commerce by giving businesses an affordable digital platform while providing customers with a better way to discover nearby opportunities.
